Sustainability at staystz

We’re working to make travel and property services more sustainable across Tanzania, supporting eco-friendly stays, responsible travel, and local communities.

Our approach

At Staystz, we believe sustainability is about making better choices— for our customers, our partners, and the environment.

We are building a platform that encourages responsible travel and supports property owners who adopt eco-friendly practices.

Our commitment

We are committed to reducing environmental impact while improving access to quality stays and services.

By working with property owners, travelers, and service providers, we aim to create a more sustainable ecosystem across Tanzania.

What we focus on

Eco-friendly properties

Supporting listings that use sustainable materials, energy-efficient systems, and responsible water usage.

Local communities

Working with local hosts and businesses to ensure tourism benefits the communities we serve.

Responsible travel

Encouraging travelers to make choices that reduce waste and protect the environment.

Looking ahead

We are continuously improving how sustainability is integrated into our platform.

Our future plans include highlighting eco-certified properties, improving transparency, and helping users make more informed choices.
