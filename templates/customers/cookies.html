Cookies Policy

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience on our platform, provide personalized content, and analyze traffic and usage patterns. Cookies help us understand how our Platform is used and ensure that it functions smoothly.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit websites. They allow the site to remember information about your visit, such as preferences and login details, to improve your experience during future visits.

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: These are necessary for the Platform to function correctly, such as maintaining your login session and ensuring secure access.
  • Performance Cookies: These help us monitor usage, understand user behavior, and improve the performance and speed of the Platform.
  • Functional Cookies: These remember your preferences, such as language, location, and personalized settings.
  • Marketing and Analytics Cookies: These help us deliver personalized content, advertisements, and measure the effectiveness of our campaigns.

How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for various purposes to make your experience on our Platform smoother and more tailored to your needs:

  • Remember your login details and account preferences.
  • Analyze traffic patterns to improve the Platform’s usability and features.
  • Personalize content, offers, and communications based on your interests.
  • Ensure security and prevent fraudulent activities.
  • Monitor performance and detect technical issues or errors.

Managing Cookies

You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. You may choose to block certain cookies, delete existing cookies, or receive alerts when a cookie is being set. Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality and performance of our Platform.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology or how we use cookies. Any updates will be posted on this page with the date of the last revision. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
