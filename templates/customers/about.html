About STAYS Tanzania

Empowering Tanzanians through smart housing, business solutions, and digital services.

Overview and Services

STAYS Tanzania was established on November 11, 2023, with a mission to assist Tanzanians through online platforms in finding rental housing, business premises, guest houses, buying and selling various products, organizing auctions, and offering flexible rent payment plans.

These plans aim to address challenges faced by landlords and tenants. Additionally, STAYS Tanzania provides loans for rent and other services.

As part of its vision, the company seeks to create employment opportunities for Tanzanian youth in every region and district.

Through our websites or the STAYS App, customers can access services at any time using a smartphone, laptop, or by making a phone call.
